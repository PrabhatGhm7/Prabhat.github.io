Research

Fixed-envelope warnings arrive late on terrain-constrained approaches.

Early CFIT detection for the terrain-constrained approach into Pokhara. Manuscript under peer review, Springer Nature.

A ground proximity warning system tests closure rate and radio altitude against a fixed envelope. On an approach hemmed in by rising terrain, that envelope is breached late and widening it brings nuisance alerts that crews learn to ignore. This work models the risk probabilistically instead.

Trajectory generation
DEM-bounded simulation over 7.48M+ state points.
Class imbalance
Rare-event modelling, not resampling.
Model
Temporal convolutional network over sequence history.
Evaluation
Held-out episodes. Lead time from first alert to contact.

Limitations, stated plainly A result is only as useful as its stated limits.

  • Simulated trajectories only. No operational flight data was used to train or evaluate the model.
  • One approach geometry at one airport. Generalisation to other terrain is not demonstrated.
  • Nuisance-alert cost in line operations is unmeasured — and it decides whether a system like this is fieldable.
